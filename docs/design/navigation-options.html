Off the Realm: where am I, how do I get back?

The Knowledge screen and the territory view take the Realm's place in the work area. The top bar, the sidebar and the hand don't change, so all that marks the switch is a small breadcrumb ("Realm › Knowledge") whose link is the only way back besides Esc. Six ways to make the switch obvious, from the least change to the most. Each is shown on Knowledge (teal) and on a territory (sage).

B · recommended

A title-block bar in the screen's colour

Every screen you navigate to opens under a full-width bar filled with its card-type colour: teal for Knowledge, sage for a territory. The bar's left end is the way home, ← Realm with its Esc keycap. Then comes the screen's name in display type, and its context (era, insight) at the right. The Realm has no bar, so if you see a bar you are somewhere else. While Knowledge is open, its top-bar button lights in the same teal, and pressing it again goes back.

  • + One rule covers every screen: a filled band means you're off the Realm, and its left end takes you back.
  • + The guide already describes this bar. §10.2 flashes "the card's type band colour … in the title block bar" during the wipe. Here the bar keeps the colour instead of flashing it, which also links the screen to the card it grew from.
  • + It is one component, ScreenHeader, so future screens get it for free.
  • − It is a large coloured plane, and §4.6 rations those. It is fine at one per screen, but the bar is the only filled plane in the header.

A sheet laid on the desk

The screen is a raised sheet with an ink edge and a hard shadow, slid in from the right but stopping short. A dimmed strip of the Realm stays visible at the left under a "‹ Realm" label, and clicking the strip goes back.

D · global

A location switch in the top bar

Knowledge leaves the button row and joins Realm in a two-position selector with lamps at the left of the top bar. The lamp shows where you are, and clicking Realm takes you home from anywhere. A territory lights Realm, because it is part of the Realm.

  • + It is always in the same place on every screen, and the console style suits a "machined" switch.
  • − A territory looks like "on the Realm", so it doesn't help in the territory case you mentioned.
  • − It moves Knowledge out of the global row, which §11.1 lays out, and it competes with the resource bar for the left end.

F · ambient

Frame the work area

A 3 px rule in the screen's colour goes round the whole work area while you're away from the Realm, with the screen's name stamped in a corner.

  • + You'd notice it in your peripheral vision without reading anything.
  • − It doesn't help with getting back. It reads as a selection or targeting state (§10.5, §11.8), and the territory box already has a sage frame, so you'd get a frame inside a frame.

Proposed pattern: the title block marks where you are

  1. The Realm is home and has no title bar. Every navigated screen gets a full-width title-block bar filled with its card-type colour (B). A coloured bar always means you're away from the Realm.
  2. The way back is always at the bar's left end. It is a real button, "← Realm", with an Esc keycap, and in a nested stack it names the parent ("← Knowledge"). The breadcrumb text moves into the bar's name.
  3. The button that opened a screen lights while it's open, and pressing it again closes the screen. Knowledge lights in teal and T, its shortcut, already toggles it. This adds part of D without a new control.
  4. Keep the wipe. The bar is what the card grows into: its colour comes from the card's type band, so the §10.2 flash now stays as the bar's colour.
