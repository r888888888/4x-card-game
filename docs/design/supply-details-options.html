Supply card details

Click a supply pile → its details → Buy

Both options reuse the existing card details modal (056): big card in the aside, rules and terms in the body, footer rule with buttons right, primary rightmost (§15.11). They differ in where the price lives. Each shows a buyable pile, then one you can't afford.

A · Price on the Buy key

The aside is the plain card. The price and copies left join the subtitle; the primary key says Buy with the wealth glyph and price, like the Play key on a hand card. When the engine refuses, the key goes dashed and its reason sits on the footer's left (not only a tooltip).

B · Price tag hangs under the card

The aside repeats the supply pile exactly: the card with its gold price tag and "6 left" below it (232), so the modal reads as "this pile, picked up". The footer key is just Buy. Refused: the tag dims as on the supply screen and the reason sits on the footer's left.
