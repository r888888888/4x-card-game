Resource icons

Candidates for food, wealth, insight and unrest, drawn to the guide's icon rules (§8): a 24 grid, one 2 px stroke scaled with size, square caps and mitred joins, circles, arcs, squares and straight lines only, five primitives at most. Each is shown large, at the resource bar's 32 px beside a value window, and at the cost plate's 20 px. The current glyph is first in each row, on a darker tile. The last section puts the recommended set beside pop, score and the card-type marks to check they stay distinct.
