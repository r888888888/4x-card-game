Ready lamps

Knowledge and Buy Cards light when you can afford something

Each option shows the top bar twice: dark (nothing you can afford, or already seen) and lit (a tech you can learn now; a supply pile you can buy now). In B–D Knowledge takes the insight hue and Buy Cards the wealth hue; the chosen A (and E's lamp) is sage on both, as the specimen's "Learn Pottery" lamp. Opening the screen puts that key's lamp out. In 288 the lamp relights only when something new becomes purchasable (rule 1). The sandbox at the bottom lets you try each look against each relight rule.
