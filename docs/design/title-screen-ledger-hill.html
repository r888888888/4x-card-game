Ledger · sun over a hill

Speed

The chosen title-screen art (backlog 213, 214): option B's ledger with the striped sun rising over one bare green hill. It rises into place when the screen opens, then rests (guide rule 4). New game brings on the day (the sun climbs); Exit sinks it into a sunset: as the sun nears the horizon it turns from gold to signal red, warm bands gather in the sky and the hill deepens. The same warmth plays at dawn. Settings leaves it be.
