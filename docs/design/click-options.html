Click options

Nine versions of ui.button.press and its ui.button.release: today's click (A) down to the darkest (F), then three two-stage clicky switches modeled on a blue-style mechanical keyboard switch (G–I). Press a key to hear the click as the face meets the desk and the tick as it comes back, with the same timing as the specimen. "×12" plays a burst at the rate a busy turn would (six presses a second) with the usual small variation between repeats. Every option goes through the interface bus's filters (high-pass at 150 Hz, −6 dB above 6 kHz) and is matched to A by energy, not by peak, so a darker click isn't simply quieter. Under each key is its brightness: the spectral centroid, where the spectrum balances. Lower reads warmer and heavier. The dashed line on each spectrum is A, for comparison.
