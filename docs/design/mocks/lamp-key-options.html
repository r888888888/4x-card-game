Latching lamp key

Lamp

Nine takes on one square push key that latches down when ON and lights a lamp. They share the same mechanism: at rest it stands on a 2 px plinth; while held it over-travels 3 px; released ON it stays latched 2 px down with its shadow hidden; released OFF it springs back. They differ in where the light is and how much of the key it takes. Press and hold one to feel the over-travel. The lamp colour is chosen at the top. The game uses 1, the window bar (item 219; the legend key, 4, was the first choice, in 182).
