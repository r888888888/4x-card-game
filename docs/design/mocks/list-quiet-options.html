Quiet selected row

Item 356

Quieter ways to mark the chosen row of a selectable list (§7.16), replacing the index card that slides 8 px out onto a hard 4 px shadow. Each demo is the Build modal's ledger sheet with the rest of item 356 in place: 24 px above each heading after the first, 24 px under the card, a thin steel scrollbar, and wheel scrolling that glides: a notch eases about 120 px and coasts to a stop (Reduce motion: it jumps). Click a row, or focus a list and use Up and Down; the list follows the selection. Names and costs are illustrative. A–F change the background; G–L, further down, add the indicator lamp.

A

Sheet strip + index tab

The chosen row lies flat in the well as a strip of sheet, and the 4 px signal tab stays on its leading edge. Everything that made it look raised is gone: the slide, the shadow and the bold weight.

The tab keeps it in the same family as a selected card (§10.5), and it can still be read at a glance in a long list.

Motion: fill 120 ms; tab wipes 120 ms machined. Sound: ui.selection as the tab lands.
B

Sheet strip alone

Only the background changes: a strip of sheet, its label inked and set semibold. No signal on the sheet at all, so the orange belongs to the Build key alone (§4.2).

It's the most literal reading of "a subtle background change". In Night the strip is only one value up from the well, so it depends on the label's weight.

Motion: fill 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection on the fill.
C

Steel strip

The same idea one step brighter: the strip is steel, the colour of a key at rest, so it reads clearly in both modes without a tab.

Since it's the colour of a key, the row can start to look like a button again, though flatter than today's.

Motion: fill 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection on the fill.
D

Sheet strip + ink tick

A as a quieter mark: the leading tab is a 2 px ink rule instead of the signal orange. It still has a precise edge, but in ink, like a ruled margin.

This frees the signal colour for the primary key. The selection no longer matches a selected card's tab.

Motion: fill 120 ms; tick wipes 120 ms machined. Sound: ui.selection.
E

Signal wash

No strip of sheet: the row takes a faint warm tint of the signal colour (12–14 %), with the tab on its edge, as if the orange tab bled into the paper.

Of the six it's the warmest and links the fill to the tab, but it's a new tinted colour (a Palette role), and orange next to the orange Build key can blur which one is the action.

Motion: tint 120 ms; tab wipes 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection.
F

Sheet strip in a hairline

B with a 1 px fine rule around the strip, like a slip of paper laid on the well. It's crisper than B in Night, and there's still no signal and no depth.

The hairline is close to the look of a bordered control, and in a dense list the rule can look busy.

Motion: fill and rule 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection.

With the indicator lamp

The guide's indicator lamp (§7.11): a 12 px disc, dark in the well with a fine ring, lit in a plane colour with a highlight. It's the guide's mark for state (a circle = an indicator, §2), so it can say "chosen" with no depth at all. Item 217 tried lamps on their own (its option D) and found them too small and too like radio buttons, so here each lamp sits on the quiet strip from A–F, or does a second job.

G

A lamp on every row

Every row has a dark lamp before its name; the chosen row's lamp lights sage, on a sheet strip. You can read the column of lamps like a bank of positions on a panel, with one lit.

This is clear in both modes, but a dozen dark discs make the list busier, and they still hint at radio buttons.

Motion: lamp on 40 ms, off 120 ms (ease.lamp); fill 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection at lamp-on.
H

Lamp on the chosen row only chosen

Only the chosen row carries a lamp, lit, as the strip fills; the other rows are text alone. The lamp takes over from the index tab, as a circle for "this one" instead of an orange bar.

It's the calmest of the six. Rows don't shift, because the lamp's space is reserved on every row.

Motion: lamp on 40 ms with the fill. Sound: ui.selection at lamp-on.
I

Status lamps

The lamps report state instead of selection: lit sage on every row the engine allows, dark on a refused one. The selection is the plain sheet strip (B).

The lamp does a real job here, so you can scan for what's buildable. The dimming of refused rows says the same thing, though, and the selection is as quiet as B's.

Motion: none on selection (lamps change only with the game). Sound: ui.selection on the fill.
J

Lamp window

The index tab becomes a lamp window, the narrow bar from the lamp key (§15.4) turned upright. Every row has one, dark, on its leading edge, and the chosen row's window lights.

It sits where the tab sat and reads as a part of the panel, not a radio button. Lit, it's quieter than the orange tab.

Motion: window on 40 ms, off 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection at lamp-on.
K

Selector panel

The lamps sit at the trailing edge in a single column, like the position lamps of a rotary selector. The chosen row's lamp is lit, on the strip.

The names stay flush left and readable. The lit lamp is next to the card it opens, which links the row to the detail, but it's far from where the eye starts reading.

Motion: lamp on 40 ms, off 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection at lamp-on.
L

Plane lamps

G, but the lamp lights in the entry's card colour: olive for a building, ochre for an upgrade, bronze for a unit. It matches the band on the card beside it.

This adds meaning without adding marks. The hues are close in Night, though, and a lamp in a card colour is new to the guide (lamps are lit in semantic hues today).

Motion: lamp on 40 ms, off 120 ms. Sound: ui.selection at lamp-on.

The same in every option (item 356)

  • Glide. A wheel notch pushes the list; the push decays (friction 8 / s), so it eases about 120 px and coasts to rest. It stops dead at the ends. Up and Down ease the selected row into view (200 ms, quart out, no overshoot). With Reduce motion, both jump.
  • Scrollbar. 8 px, square, steel on the well; ink-2 under the pointer and while dragged.
  • Air. 24 px above a heading that follows rows (Buildings → Upgrades → Units); the first heading still opens the well. 24 px between the card and the lines under it.
  • Unchanged. Hover only inks the label; focus keeps its teal ring; a refused row is dimmed with its reason.
