Renewal · options

Backlog 255: under Anarchy, renewal trashes cards from your hand, deck or discard. Every card is listed on its own (no stacks), sorted by name so the draw order stays hidden, and nothing is trashed until you confirm. Each demo is the Egypt starting library (11 cards) on Anarchy's 2nd turn: trash 2. Click cards to choose; the confirm key arms at 2 of 2. Esc or Close isn't offered: renewal is owed. B is chosen; its sounds are live (click once anywhere to wake the audio), and the plan for them is under it.

A

Card spread

The cards as they look in the hand, in one wrapping grid. A click lifts a card (the hand's chosen pose: 12 px up, the index tab out) and stamps it Trash; a second click puts it back. The footer counts on segmented progress and the confirm key arms only at the full count.

Closest to today's screen and the hand. Reads well up to ~16 cards; a 30-card library scrolls.

B

Ledger · chosen

A selectable list (§7.16): one row per card, its type mark, name and cost. Hover or arrow keys pull a row out like an index card and show its plain face on the right (only the row says whether it's chosen); Enter or a click lights the row's lamp and strikes the name through. Compact at any library size and the most keyboard-friendly.

Like the Knowledge sheet and the identity modal's lists. Scales to any deck; less visual than A.

C

Trash tray

Compact cards (the Realm's one-line size) above a well with one dashed slot per card owed. A click moves the card into the next slot, leaving its gap in the grid; a click on a slotted card sends it back. You see exactly what's going before you confirm.

The most physical. The count is the slots; the tray grows with the count (5 on turn 5).
