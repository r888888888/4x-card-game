What a tech gives

Knowledge screen → a tech's details → the cards it gives

Today a tech's details say "Granary can now be bought in the supply." but never what a Granary does. Techs give 0–3 cards (13 give one, 6 give two, 3 give three: Mysticism, Priesthood, Code of Laws); some are wonders or governments (Kingship, Theocracy). Each option is shown with Pottery (two cards), and the recommended one also with Mysticism (three) and with a given card opened on top. The card text is the engine's real generated text.

Recommendation: B, inline compact cards that open their details. You see every given card at a glance in the game's own card language (cost, type, rules), with no click; the click is only for the deeper layer (the "How it works" terms, flavour), and it uses the modal stack that already exists, so Esc returns to the tech. Three compact cards (245 px, the tableau size) fit the modal's width.

A · Card names are links

The rules lines stay as they are; each given card's name becomes a link (and a row of chips under the rules, so a keyboard can Tab to them). A click opens that card's details over the tech's.

For
  • Smallest change; nothing new on screen.
  • Scales to any number of cards.
Against
  • Still hides what the card does behind a click per card.
  • Links inside RichText are new to the UI; focus and Tab need their own work.

B · Inline compact cards Recommended

A Gives row spans the modal under the body: each given card as its compact face (the tableau size), with a caption saying how you get it ("1 to your discard · in the supply" or "in the supply"). The "can now be bought" rules lines it repeats leave the Rules list. Each card is a button: click, Enter or I opens its full details on top; Esc comes back. A tech that gives nothing has no row.

For
  • Answers "what does it do" with no click.
  • Uses the existing card face and modal stack.
  • The Rules list gets shorter, not longer.
Against
  • The modal grows ~210 px taller.
  • Compact faces show one line per field; the long tail (terms) is a click away.

C · Given cards stacked in the aside

The aside under the tech card holds its given cards at compact size, a column of paper. The body is unchanged.

For
  • The body text is untouched.
  • Reads as "this tech, and what comes with it".
Against
  • With three cards the aside is ~900 px tall and the modal scrolls.
  • Two different card sizes side by side in one column.

D · Text section per card

A Gives section in the body: each card's name, type and cost, then its rules lines, as text. Its terms merge into the tech's "How it works".

For
  • Pure text; fits the RichText body as it is.
  • Shows everything, terms included, no click.
Against
  • Doesn't look like cards; the reader translates.
  • Long: three cards plus their terms is a wall of text.
