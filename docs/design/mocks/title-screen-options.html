Title screen options

Backlog 213

Three designs for the title screen and its large-format button. Each mock is a 16:9 window drawn at scale; press the buttons to feel the travel. New game is the primary key (signal fill) in every option. Pick one (or mix: a layout from one, a button from another) and 213 gets its criteria from it.

A Front plate

A centred title block between a heavy and a fine rule, over one column of wide keys. Each key is the End turn key's family: steel fill, 3 px ink border, 4 px plinth, caps label, and a keycap index (1, 2, 3) on a well inset that doubles as its keyboard shortcut.
