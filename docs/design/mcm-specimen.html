The game's own 1920 × 1080 layout, scaled to fit, on walnut grain under the board and the strip (341). The strip runs across the top, its keys at the right: Buy Cards and Knowledge carry a ready lamp (lit when something new can be bought or learned; it goes out when the screen opens), and Log a bullet while it holds unread lines. The Realm's row (events first, then the frontier and the territories) and the hand share the play area; the rail at the right holds the civilization, its government as a small caps link, and End turn at its foot, with the reason under it when the turn can't end. Notices come out of the rail's edge as flags. Click a card to select it, or press End turn: its plate and the strip's flap to the next turn while the counters roll.

Controls

§15.1–15.4, §7.16 · press, tab rail, lamp key, list

Knowledge

A key's ready lamp (288) lights with the starburst when something new becomes affordable, and goes out when its screen opens: press the key. The divider tab (241) is the way back from a screen: the board's colour, its right edge slanted, at the left end of the screen's title bar.

Press moves the face 2 px into its shadow in 70 ms and comes back in 120 ms. No scale, no ripple. Every key sits on a clicky key switch: its snap at actuation, then the bottom-out as the face meets the desk (≈ 38 ms); a quieter snap and top-out on the way back. Entering a key gives a soft hover tick, 8 dB under the press (245). The disabled key doesn't move, is silent on hover and gives one dead tap; the link is printed, so it makes no sound.

The selectable list (§7.16): rows rest flat in the well; the selected one is marked in place, with no depth: a sheet strip and a lit lamp before its name (356). The other rows keep the lamp's room, so no name moves. Focus is not selection: the focus ring and the lamp never mean the same thing. Up and Down move the selection.

Resource strip

§11.2 · plate, odometer, forecast

Each changed digit column rolls in 60 ms steps (up for gain, down for loss, at most 8 steps), ticking as each digit lands; the last step registers instead. The roll is the only mark of a change: no tag beside the figure, so nothing after it moves. The quiet figure beside each is next upkeep's change. Unrest's limit is in its tooltip, not the strip: its glyph breathes when the next upkeep would reach it, and its figure turns danger once it's there.

Cards

§15.6 · hand, Realm, supply, identity

Every card is printed on paper (341) and rests on a soft shadow; hover slides it 4 px up as the shadow grows, with the soft hover tick; selecting pulls it 12 px out of the row with the orange index tab, which clips on with a barely-there tick. A card you can't play greys its background, border and band, never its text, says why in a strip at its foot, and snaps sideways (±4 px) when you try to play it, one low tap per stop. In the Realm's row a card shows one line per field: a settled territory its city's name, the territory under it, then its live slots, housing, free workers and defence, with a warning plate when a raid strikes next turn; an event's badge carries its state at the right (turns left, or a raid's strength against the defence in danger); a frontier is dashed over hatching, like unmapped land, and a badge names what it is. A unit is a card on the bronze plane.

Notices come out of the rail as flags (250): a sheet strip with its priority's glyph and hue bar on the rail's side and a ×; info and caution go after 6 s, urgent ones stay until dismissed. A modal shows a card's face beside its text (details, events, raids, 271) with its keys at the foot; a footer with one primary key shows it in signal orange with a 3 px ink border, last (251), and an event's options are plain keys, neither one favoured.

Screens

§11.3 · title bar, Knowledge, a territory, the supply
Knowledge · the techs by era

Knowledge

Turn 5 · Stone Age
Insight 4 · play a Research card for more

A screen you navigate to opens under a title bar filled with its colour; the divider tab at its left end, "◂ Realm", is the way back. On Knowledge each era is a band beside its tiles, whose fill says their state: researched in the teal plane and ticked, available in ink with its cost, a met eureka ticked under its name, locked in the well with what it needs. Hover a tile you can learn: its prerequisite and the techs it opens take a gold border. An era not reached lies under vellum that says when it opens. The motion of every screen is in transitions.html.

A territory · in place of the Realm

Uruk

A click on a territory opens its view (101) under a sage bar: its city's name over the territory, its keywords, the live line and a pop pip per housing, lit for each pop. Build… opens the build modal (297: a selectable list beside the selected entry's card and its flavor, 354); Rename… names the city (248). Its slots hold the buildings' cards; an empty slot is a "+ Build" tile that opens the same modal. The city, the buildings and an enabled empty slot ink their border under the pointer, with the soft hover tick (342).

Sound

§14.1, §16 · tokens, levels, repetition

Press a token to hear it alone. Every sound above is one of these, synthesized here as a placeholder and normalized to its level (0 dB = −26 dBFS peak).

Repetition test (§16.8)

Very-high-frequency tokens repeat 6 times a second for 4 s, high 2 a second, occasional once a second. A sound passes if you stop noticing it after a minute and it never grates; if it fails, shorten it, lower it, simplify it, or make it silent.
