Controls

§15.1–15.4 · press, tab rail, rocker

Press moves the face 2 px into its shadow in 70 ms and comes back in 120 ms. No scale, no ripple.

Resource bar

§11.2 · odometer, delta tag, lamp

Each changed digit column rolls in 60 ms steps (up for gain, down for loss, at most 8 steps). The tag snaps in, holds 600 ms and wipes out. The lamp pulses once; at a limit it stays lit and the caption says so.

Cards

§15.6 · index cards, slide out of the row

Hover slides a card 4 px up with a plinth shadow; selecting pulls it 12 px out of the row with the orange index tab. The dimmed card snaps sideways (±4 px) when you try to play it.
